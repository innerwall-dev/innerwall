## Viz dependency: `@xyflow/react`, laid out by `@dagrejs/dagre`

ADR-0008 already records the library (ReactFlow, of which `@xyflow/react` is the current package) and the label-group model. In session this was ruled to stand, so the choice was measured against the stated capabilities rather than reopened:

- **Custom node rendering.** Nodes are console components: an SVG frame with the tokens' fill, stroke, and dash geometry, and HTML text in the design's type. Edges are custom SVG paths with token color and dash, arrowheads per decision, and HTML label chips.
- **Programmatic layout.** Positions are supplied by the console; the library only renders them. The layout is layered left to right by `@dagrejs/dagre`, with unmanaged peers pinned to the first layer. A layer taller than the map is wide wraps into columns, so a group that reaches hundreds of others still fits at a legible zoom. Chips are placed where edges leave their source and pushed apart where they would overlap.
- **Smooth pan, zoom, and selection at estate scale.** Measured on `--estate-extra 300` (314 groups, 328 edges), headless Chromium at 1440×900 in this container:
  - First edge drawn 0.98 s after navigation, including the four rollups, the walk of 929 workloads, and the layout.
  - Pan at fit: p95 frame 16.7 ms. Zoom: p95 16.7 ms, max 33 ms. Pan zoomed in: p95 16.8 ms.
  - Only elements in view are mounted, and label chips give way below half zoom (unreadable there; the edges stay selectable). DOM nodes met the frame budget at this scale, so canvas-class rendering was not needed.
- **Cost.** The map loads as its own chunk: 269 kB (88 kB gzip). The main bundle stays at 438 kB. The library's structural custom properties point at the tokens (`src/index.css`); nothing it draws has a color of its own. Attribution is hidden.

## Escalations (maintainer rulings, this session)

1. **Design package not in the session.** Attached by the maintainer. `tokens.css` matches the package's names and values exactly.
2. **ADR-0008 vs. the brief.** ADR-0008 fixes the library and label-group aggregation; the brief left the library open and pointed at per-host `src,dst`. **Ruled:** ReactFlow with label groups, folded client-side; no ADR.
3. **Node mode, member counts, reporting count, and the dropped warning** need the workload list, while M3.5 ruled "omit rather than walk". **Ruled:** walk `/workloads` scoped by the map's filters.
4. **Draw a rule.** **Ruled:** show peers and services, keep the button inert, omit the ruleset and the count.
5. **Services on a group edge.** **Ruled:** pairs, then flows. No client-side rollup across pairs.
6. **Design disagrees with itself on geometry.** The legend draws observed dashes as `2 3`, would-block as `7 4`, and blocked at 2.5px; the edges and tokens use `2 4`, `8 4`, and 2px. **Ruled:** tokens everywhere, with volume scaled 1.5→2px as above.

Decisions made in session, open to review:
- The chip shows the prevailing decision's own count.
- The scope filters destinations (the surface's `label` semantics). Sources outside the scope still appear. The suggested rule adds the scope's requirements only when every source carries them.
- A "no ‹key› label" group sits alongside "unlabeled". The design shows only "unlabeled", which is true only of workloads with no labels at all.
- The estate seed (commit 4) is outside the brief's list. The base review fleet has no `app` label, so without it the composites could not show label groups. It is development data built through the store's own writes and ingest's resolution.
- The harness's `freshInstall` gains the address-group and empty-rollup routes the map now reads.
